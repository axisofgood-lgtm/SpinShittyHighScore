Spin Sh*tty – a laundromat minigame by Axis of Good

Spin Sh*tty is a free retro pixel-art arcade minigame made for laundromat moments: the few minutes you spend waiting while your laundry spins. No download and no sign-up. It plays right in your phone's browser.

How to play

Clock in for a 3-minute shift at the most broken laundromat in Brooklyn. Set each dial, hit start, kick or tap the machines that act up, and keep the customers' patience from running out. Squirrels move into empty machines, and a golden gym sock sometimes makes a run for it. Fast fixes build your streak and your score.

Worldwide top 10

Enter your initials at the end of your shift and see if you make the worldwide high score board.

Made in Bed-Stuy

Spin Sh*tty: A Laundromat of Regret was made in Bedford-Stuyvesant (Bed-Stuy), Brooklyn by Axis of Good, an independent product studio.
